### Why the Black Pleated Wide doesn't make this trip

1. It only reaches `smart` 1–2, so it is **locked out of all five casual/travel
   slots** — and long-trousers-on-flights makes those slots *more* important.
2. Its best pairing, Wine Polo + Pleated (a pick), scores **86**. But Wine Polo +
   Beige Linen scores **104** and Navy LS + Camel scores **96** — also picks. It
   loses to better combos of the same tops.
3. Substituting it for Camel Chinos **breaks the plan: 13/14, total 1254.**

It is a good garment with no slot on *this* itinerary. On a city trip with three
bar nights and no beach it would start.
