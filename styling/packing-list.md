## Laundry — this is what makes 14 items work

8 tops cover 14 wears, so **6 wears come from washing**. Without laundry,
even an 18-item kit only covers 10 of 14 slots.

- **Drop 1 — Bangkok, morning of Aug 19.** Same-day hotel service. Returns
  Rustic Button-down, Wine Polo, Navy LS/white stripes for Aug 21–24.
- **Drop 2 — Hanoi, evening of Aug 22.** Returns Navy Striped and Wine Polo
  for Aug 23–24.

**Do not plan to sink-wash.** Phu Quoc averages **86.5% relative humidity** in
August; cotton and linen will not dry overnight. Use hotel/villa service.

## Verified constraints (sources checked, not assumed)

- **Grand Palace** prohibits "short hot pants or short pants" and sleeveless
  tops → **long trousers, Aug 18**. Knee-length shorts do *not* comply.
  Sandals are not addressed either way.
- **Lebua Sky Bar** explicitly permits "tailored shorts, loafers, or elegant
  sandals". Only flip-flops, beach slippers, sleeveless, swimwear and athletic
  wear are banned. It is *not* a closed-shoe venue.
- **TRIBE** permits shorts and sandals by day; smart casual at night is a
  recommendation, not a footwear rule.
- **Go-karting (EasyKart)** requires closed shoes — but **rents them for 50 THB**,
  so it should not drive your packing.
- **ONYX, Tichuca, Aether** publish no findable dress code. This is the main
  reason to carry the Onitsuka.
- **Shooting range** publishes no footwear requirement.
- **Halong Bay** operators only *recommend* trainers; nothing is mandatory.
