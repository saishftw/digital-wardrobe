# The judged plan — Bangkok / Phu Quoc / Hanoi, 17–24 Aug 2026

Built by judgement, not by the optimiser. Where this differs from `packing-list.md`,
this file is the recommendation and the difference is explained below.

## Where the pipeline was wrong

1. **The `+12` pick bonus dominates.** Scores span 85–120, so a single grade
   outweighs every material, colour and climate term combined. The optimiser
   can only re-rank combos you already blessed; it cannot propose.
2. **No concept of putting a good piece at risk.** It sent the Striped Camp
   Collar kayaking at Halong because linen scores well on quick-dry — ignoring
   that you do not take your best shirt into a kayak.
3. **No sun or sweat model.** It put Navy Striped in direct midday sun at the
   Grand Palace. Navy absorbs heat and shows sweat; that walk is 3+ hours on
   open stone at 33 °C.
4. **No mosquito model.** Phu Quoc, August, evening, feet in sand — that is a
   long-sleeve slot, and nothing in the scoring says so.
5. **Laundry is invisible to it.** It spaces re-wears by "3 slots" regardless of
   the Aug 19 Bangkok and Aug 22 Hanoi laundry drops, which reset everything.
6. **The flight home is not a fashion slot.** Aug 24 you see nobody. It should
   absorb the most-worn items, and the optimiser scored it like any other day.
7. **It never used the Sambas.** 14/14 slots in cork sandals made them dead
   weight — but the real answer is that six slots genuinely want a closed shoe.

## The four calls I'd defend hardest

**Rustic + Pleated at Lebua, not the Wine Polo.** You said this is the pairing
you want and I agree with you over the machine. Rust on black reads deliberate;
a polo and beige linen reads resort. Lebua is the one venue on the trip with an
enforced door, and the pleated has exactly one correct night — this is it.

**Camp Collar at the Grand Palace.** Off-white reflects, the slubby texture
moves air, the collar covers you respectfully. Beige linen will crease and that
is fine — a temple is the right place to spend the linen's one weakness.

**Jäger tee on the water.** Its job is not to win an outfit, it is to take the
salt, chlorine and sun so the camp collar doesn't. That is why it packs.

**Camel Chinos are the travel trouser, all four flights.** Wrinkle 6 vs the
beige linen's 3, and laundry on Aug 19 and Aug 22 means Aug 24 flies on a clean
pair. This removes the beige-linen-on-a-flight problem entirely.
